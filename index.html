a personal life redesign experiment

me, in progress.

What if I treated my life like a design project?

hi, it's me

As a UX designer, I think about how people interact with products: what frustrates them, what could work better, and how small changes improve an experience.

But what happens when I apply that same thinking to myself?

This is a little experiment in understanding how I spend my time, questioning some of my habits, imagining different possibilities, and testing small changes. No perfect solutions, no five-year master plan. Just going with the flow ~

something I've been thinking about

02

Bugs in my operating system

Every system has its bugs. Mine is no different.

BUG 001

Everything needs to be productive.

Sometimes I feel like I need to be doing something useful all the time. Even when I'm taking a break, I feel like I should be doing something else.

BUG 002

I'll do it later.

I sometimes put off things I genuinely want to do because just coz.

BUG 003

I need to have everything figured out.

There's this pressure to know exactly where I'm going, what I want to do, and how everything is supposed to work out.

GRAVITY PROBLEM

Not enough hours in a day.

Between classes, projects, responsibilities, and everything else, time is a real constraint. I can't magically create more hours, but I can rethink how I use the ones I have.

these are a starting point for reflection, not problems I expect to fix overnight.

04

Life experiments: version 1.0

Instead of making huge life changes, I want to experiment with small things and see what actually makes a difference.

EXPERIMENT 001

The No-Productivity Day

The idea: Spend some time doing things without worrying about whether they're productive.

Testing: Can I enjoy doing nothing particularly useful?

EXPERIMENT 002

The Spontaneous Explorer

The idea: Stop waiting for the perfect time to try something new.

Testing: Does making room for spontaneous experiences make everyday life more exciting? (I think it 100% does)

EXPERIMENT 003

The Creative Reset

The idea: Make time for creativity outside of academic and professional work.

Testing: Can I enjoy being creative without having an end goal?

05

Still under construction 🚧

I don't think designing a better life means having everything figured out.

Sometimes it just means questioning routines, trying something different, and realizing that there isn't one correct way to do things. Basically, freeballing it.

Maybe the goal isn't to find the perfect version of myself. Maybe it's to keep experimenting until I find more ways to enjoy the process.
